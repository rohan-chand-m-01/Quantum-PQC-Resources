# Phase 2: Classical Cryptography Foundations

Understand the classical systems (RSA, ECC, Diffie-Hellman) to comprehend why they are vulnerable to quantum attacks.

## Recommended Books

- **Serious Cryptography**
  - Author: Jean-Philippe Aumasson
  - [Chapter Previews](https://nostarch.com/seriouscrypto)

- **Applied Cryptography**
  - Author: Bruce Schneier
  - [Online Access](https://www.schneier.com/books/applied-cryptography/)

- **Cryptography: Theory and Practice**
  - Authors: Stinson & Paterson
  - [Springer Access](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1201/9781315282497)
