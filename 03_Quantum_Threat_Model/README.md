# Phase 3: Quantum Threat Model

Resources to understand Quantum Computing and the specific threat it poses to modern cryptography.

## Key Resources

- **Quantum Computation and Quantum Information**
  - Authors: Nielsen & Chuang
  - [Cambridge Access](https://www.cambridge.org/9781107002173)

- **Quantum Computing Since Democritus**
  - Author: Scott Aaronson
  - [Lecture Notes](https://www.scottaaronson.com/democritus/)

- **Lecture Notes on Quantum Algorithms**
  - Author: Andrew Childs
  - [PDF (UMD)](https://www.cs.umd.edu/~amchilds/qa/qa.pdf)

## Key Algorithms
- **Shor's Algorithm**: Breaks RSA/ECC.
- **Grover's Algorithm**: Affects symmetric cryptography (AES).
